If you're starting to sell bento cakes and can't decide which flavour to lead with, here's a quick side-by-side of the ten we make on order. Each of them earned its place — no filler on the list.

## The starter three: what to lead with

**[Oreo](/en/recipes/oreo-bento-cake/)** — best-seller. Chocolate sponge, cream cheese, cookie crumb, cherry coulis. Deep flavour, forgiving assembly, ingredients from any supermarket. If you're taking your first order, take an Oreo order.

**[Raffaello](/en/recipes/raffaello-bento-cake/)** — the one that gets ordered for anniversaries, weddings and 8 March. Vanilla sponge, coconut-almond cream, whole Raffaello candy on top. Higher cost of goods than Oreo, but the price you can charge is 30–40% higher.

**[Red Velvet](/en/recipes/red-velvet-bento-cake/)** — cocoa sponge with the signature red crumb and cream-cheese frosting. Instagram-friendly cross-section: buyers want the video slice. Requires a real red food colouring, so first order costs a bit more.

## The chocolate three

**[Snickers](/en/recipes/snickers-bento-cake/)** — chocolate sponge, salted caramel, peanut layer, chocolate mirror. Rich, filling, ordered as a "gift for men" more often than any other flavour. Peanut butter needs to be smooth, not the crunchy kind.

**[Ferrero Rocher](/en/recipes/ferrero-rocher-bento-cake/)** — chocolate-hazelnut. The most premium chocolate flavour in the collection: Nutella-style filling, whole Ferrero Rocher on top, gold accents. Cost of goods is highest here.

**[Cinnabon](/en/recipes/cinnabon-bento-cake/)** — cinnamon roll turned cake. Warm-spice sponge, cream-cheese frosting, thin caramel spiral. The only "cosy" flavour in the collection — sells best in October–December.

## The fruit three

**[Pistachio-Raspberry](/en/recipes/pistachio-raspberry-bento-cake/)** — the "grown-up" flavour. Pistachio sponge, cream-cheese with real pistachio paste, raspberry confit. Pistachio paste is the expensive part; a starter can substitute with pistachio praline (cheaper) but it changes the flavour.

**[Poppy-Citrus](/en/recipes/poppy-seed-citrus-bento-cake/)** — a lighter option for people who don't want chocolate. Poppy-seed sponge, orange curd, light citrus cream. Great for spring orders.

**[Cherry Chocolate](/en/recipes/cherry-chocolate-bento-cake/)** — classic "cherry-cognac" flavour re-cast as a bento. Chocolate sponge, cherry compote, cream cheese. A Ukrainian bakery staple, works well for older clients.

## The wildcard

**[Pina Colada](/en/recipes/pina-colada-bento-cake/)** — coconut sponge, pineapple confit, whipped white-chocolate cream. Summer only: the flavour reads flat in cold weather but is unbeatable at a beach-adjacent celebration.

## Which one to make first

Look at your first order, not your favourite. The person paying you has a picture in their head — meet it. If they haven't specified, offer Oreo. It's the flavour with the highest conversion rate and the least room to fail.
