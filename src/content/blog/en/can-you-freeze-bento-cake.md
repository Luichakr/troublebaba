The freezer question comes from two directions. A client wants to buy the cake now and eat it next week. A baker wants to take load off the day before a big order. These are two different questions, and confusing them is the expensive mistake.

## What cold does to cream

Dairy cream is an emulsion: fat holds water inside a structure. In the freezer that water becomes ice crystals, and the crystals tear the structure.

On thawing, part of the moisture never goes back into the structure — it weeps out. The cream turns grainy and wet, and the wall stops holding. That is not "bad thawing" but an irreversible change: an emulsion cannot be reassembled.

The more free water a cream contains, the stronger the effect. Fat-based creams like ganache take the cold better; wet ones — mascarpone, curd and dairy creams — take it worst.

## Why an assembled cake isn't frozen

Besides the cream, three more things in the cake don't survive freezing:

- **fresh berries** — they turn to juice on thawing and wash the cream out;
- **finishing decor** — chocolate elements and lettering crack from the temperature swing;
- **the surface** — moisture from the air condenses on the cold cake as it thaws, and the cake literally gets wet.

This is why professional work freezes components rather than results — and does it by specific rules for a specific filling, not as a general habit.

## What to tell the client

"Can I freeze it if we don't finish it?" comes up regularly. The answer should be short and not alarmist:

> Technically you can, but the cake will be different afterwards: the cream loses its smoothness and fresh berries lose their look. Better to collect the cake closer to the date — it keeps comfortably for two days in the fridge.

You haven't said no — you explained the consequence and offered a working alternative. A client who gets grainy cream out of their own freezer will still write to you, not to themselves. So this belongs at the order stage.

If the client insists, put the agreement in writing. One sentence — "note that the cream texture will change after freezing" — closes the complaint question entirely.
