Packaging is half the impression of a bento cake. Considered presentation raises the perceived value and drives repeat orders. Let's lay out the basic kit and what to keep in mind.

## The basic packaging kit

- **Disposable kraft lunch box** — the base of a bento presentation.
- **Greaseproof paper**, 15×15 or 18×18 cm — lines the bottom so the cake doesn't stick.
- **Ribbon and a candle** — small details that turn a cake into a gift.
- **Kraft bag** — so the client has an easy way to carry it.

## Branding

Add brand elements: a sticker, a business card, or a tag with your Instagram and phone number. It's free advertising — the box gets seen by the client's friends and guests.

## What to tell the client

Send a short instruction with the cake (SMS or a tag works fine):

- storage conditions (fridge, for how many hours or days);
- transport rules (keep flat, don't turn upside down);
- when it tastes best.

This cuts the number of “cake collapsed / melted” complaints and shows care.

## Why it matters for business

The client is paying not only for the flavour but for the feeling of a gift. Considered packaging is what separates a “cake from home” from a product you can charge full price for without apology.

## Transport: what matters

A 12 cm bento fits in a 13×13×8 cm box with a bit of headroom at the top. That headroom is critical — if the cake touches the lid, any jolt in the car imprints the decor on it. Build the cake **1–1.5 cm shorter** than the box height.

Cake life in transit depends on the cream and temperature. Details in the piece on [how long a bento cake lasts](/en/blog/bento-cake-shelf-life/).

## FAQ

**Where to source boxes in bulk?**
Most countries have local food-service packaging suppliers (search: “kraft lunch box wholesale”). Minimum orders are usually 50–100 units. Per-box price at wholesale drops 2–3× vs. retail.

**Do I need a separate box for the candle and ribbon?**
No. The candle and ribbon go inside the main box, next to the cake. Just wrap them in a piece of parchment or a paper pouch so they don't touch the cream.

**Should I use clear boxes with a window?**
It's a stylistic call. Clear boxes look good in social feeds but read as less premium. Kraft plus a minimalist sticker works better for a high-end positioning.
