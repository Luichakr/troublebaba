A first bento cake usually doesn't come out the way it looks in photos — and that is normal. It is rarely the recipe: more often a beginner has misjudged how long it takes and how difficult a cake to begin with.

## What size to start with

12 cm. It is big enough to work on comfortably by hand and standard enough that a box, a board and a ring are easy to find in any baking supply shop.

Smaller sizes — 8–10 cm — look simpler but are harder to work: less room to move your hand. More in [bento cake size](/en/blog/bento-cake-size/).

## How much time to allow

The main beginner's mistake is assuming a cake gets made in one evening. Realistically it is **4–5 hours of actual work spread over two days**.

Spread out is the key word. Between stages the cake sits in the fridge, and nothing compresses that time: not experience, not a better mixer. An experienced baker wins on the handwork, not on the chilling.

Hence the practical conclusion for a first order: don't take a cake "for tomorrow". Take it for the day after.

## Start with one flavour

The fewer variables in a first attempt, the clearer it is where things went wrong. One sponge, one cream, no filling, no fondant and no mirror glaze — and one clean decorative element instead of five bad ones.

An elaborate cake in someone else's photo is usually their twentieth cake, not their first.

## When you can start taking orders

The marker is simple: when the same cake has come out **the same 3–4 times running** — shape, taste and the look of the cut. This is not about perfection but about predictability: you should know how long it will take and what you'll get before you begin.

It makes sense to take the first orders from friends, or at a "testing" discount. That removes the pressure and lets you run the whole cycle including delivery — [how to transport a bento cake](/en/blog/how-to-transport-bento-cake/).

## A plan for the first week

1. Make one cake for yourself — no client, no deadline.
2. Repeat the same cake twice more and compare the three against each other.
3. Take a first test order from someone you know.
4. Only then work out your costs and set a price — [how much a bento cake costs](/en/blog/bento-cake-cost/).
