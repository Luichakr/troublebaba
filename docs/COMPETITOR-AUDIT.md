# Competitor audit — troublebaba.com vs professional international PDF-cookbook landings

_Generated on handover day. Sources verified. Includes prioritized gap list, above-the-fold audit, trust-bootstrap options for a no-testimonial launch, and copy patterns worth stealing._

## Executive summary — 5 highest-impact fixes

1. **Show the price 7-9 times, not 3-4.** Rule: any major section break → price + CTA nearby.
2. **Above-the-fold has zero trust signal.** In the first 800px add: author portrait + `100k+ Instagram` badge + micro-trust chips (`66 pages · PDF · instant`) alongside the CTA.
3. **Instagram social proof via embed + author backstory** — the strongest trust element available to a new-launch creator without formal testimonials.
4. **Attach a real date to the `-33%` anchor**, not perpetual. CR drops within a week if the anchor never ends.
5. **CTA verbs must carry the price**: `Get the book — 800 ₴` instead of `Buy PDF`. Every button.

## A · Price-display frequency audit

**troublebaba.com currently:** ~3-4 price mentions (nav-buy, hero, price card, sticky mobile).

**Professional standard:** 7-9 price+CTA pairs, one after each major content block.

**Recommended insertion points** (in reading order):
1. Nav / hero (already there)

4. After PDF preview grid — reminder card
5. After guarantee block — anchor + Buy
6. After FAQ — final CTA
7. Sticky mobile (already there)
8. Optional: sticky desktop bottom-bar

## D · Trust bootstrap — 5 elements real for TROUBLEBABA

Since we have no formal reviews/testimonials at launch:

1. **Instagram social proof block** — follower badge + last-9-posts embed. Strongest available trust.
2. **Author story + photo** — "UA pastry blogger, 5+ years, 100k+ IG followers, still baking bento cakes to sell every week".
3. **Process transparency** — "Every recipe rewritten 3+ times · Tested on European-market ingredients · Diameter-conversion table for any pan size".
4. **Reader-repost gallery** — as buyers start posting their attempts, add real IG screenshots (only with owner's consent — no personal names).
5. **Sample recipe visible from homepage** — currently `/free-recipe/` is buried. Surface it near the CTA as "peek at the writing style".

## Pricing context 2025-2026

- `$20 / 10 recipes` sits inside the normal `$15-29` range for indie PDF cookbooks.
- `-33%` anchor works only with a **windowed launch date** — perpetual anchors decay in CR within a week.
- "One-time payment, yours forever" is now a **required anti-subscription-fatigue framing**.
- No-refund clause is fine when balanced with a warm result guarantee. Both should appear near every CTA.

## Caveats

Direct indie-blogger-selling-PDF-on-own-site examples are relatively scarce in the open web — most large names (Broma, Half Baked Harvest, Sally's) sell physical books via Amazon, not self-published PDFs. Adlard sells courses. The above patterns therefore blend PDF-cookbook conventions with wider digital-product landing best practice; individual tactics validated against multiple sources are safer bets than a single citation.
